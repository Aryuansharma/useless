# ☕ Sunny Side Café — Business Website

A modern, responsive website concept for a neighborhood café and bakery, designed to showcase its menu, brand identity, customer experience, and essential business information.

Built with **HTML5 and custom CSS**, the website provides a welcoming digital storefront where visitors can explore menu highlights, learn about the café, read customer testimonials, and find contact details and visiting hours.

The project also incorporates foundational SEO techniques and Schema.org structured data to demonstrate how a small-business website can be designed with discoverability and usability in mind.

## ✨ Features

- **Business Landing Page:** A welcoming hero section with a headline, introductory content, and clear calls to action.
- **About Section:** Highlights the café's coffee, bakery offerings, and community atmosphere.
- **Menu Showcase:** Presents featured drinks, breakfast items, and baked goods in an organized layout.
- **Visit Information:** Displays business hours, location details, telephone number, and email address.
- **Customer Testimonials:** Provides a dedicated section for customer feedback and social proof.
- **Responsive Design:** Uses a separate CSS stylesheet to support layouts across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices, depending on the implemented CSS.
- **SEO Metadata:** Includes a page title, meta description, keywords, author metadata, and Open Graph tags.
- **Structured Data:** Uses JSON-LD with the Schema.org `CafeOrCoffeeShop` type to describe the business to search engines.
- **Clickable Contact Details:** Includes `tel:` and `mailto:` links for convenient communication.
- **Custom Favicon:** Uses an embedded SVG favicon without requiring a separate image file.
- **Semantic HTML:** Organizes page content with elements such as `header`, `main`, `section`, `article`, and `footer`.

## 🗂️ Website Sections

### 1. Hero — First Impression

Introduces Sunny Side Café with a prominent headline, descriptive text, and two calls to action: exploring the menu and planning a visit.

### 2. About Us — Brand Introduction

Communicates the café's concept through descriptions of artisan coffee, freshly prepared bakery products, and a welcoming community space.

### 3. Menu Favorites — Featured Products

Displays four sample menu items:

- Signature Latte
- Breakfast Sandwich
- Blueberry Muffin
- Cold Brew

Each item includes a short description to help visitors understand the offering.

### 4. Plan Your Visit — Business Information

Presents the sample address, opening hours, telephone number, and email address. A placeholder area is reserved for an embedded map or location image.

### 5. Testimonials — Customer Experience

Features two sample customer testimonials to demonstrate how reviews and customer feedback can be incorporated into a business website.

### 6. Footer — Business Identity

Provides the café name, address, and a closing message that reinforces its neighborhood-focused brand identity.

## 🔍 SEO and Structured Data

The project includes several SEO-related elements:

- A descriptive HTML title and meta description.
- Open Graph title, description, type, URL, and image metadata.
- A JSON-LD business entity using Schema.org's `CafeOrCoffeeShop` type.
- Business contact details and opening-hours information.
- Semantic HTML sections that organize the page content.

These elements demonstrate basic on-page SEO implementation. Actual search visibility depends on many additional factors, including content quality, site performance, indexing, and the accuracy of published business information.
