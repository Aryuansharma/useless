About Us

Local coffee shop with a welcoming vibe

Sunny Side Café is your neighborhood destination for handcrafted coffee, freshly baked treats, and a calm place to gather, work, or unwind. We source beans from local roasters and bake our pastries each morning for maximum freshness.
